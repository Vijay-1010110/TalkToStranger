
4. Status will change to a green **"Success"** — Google will crawl and index your site automatically within 24 to 72 hours.

### Step 5: Submit to Bing & DuckDuckGo Webmaster Tools
1. Go to [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters).
2. Click **Import from Google Search Console**.
3. All verification and sitemaps are imported in 1 click! This gets you indexed on **Bing, Yahoo, DuckDuckGo, and Microsoft Copilot**.
